# WRK-001 — Remote, Hybrid, and Flexible Work

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document defines Meridian's work arrangements, the expectations attached to each, working hours and overlap requirements, the rules for working temporarily from another country, the process for permanently relocating, and the home office stipend.

### 1.2 Related Documents
Salary consequences of a location change are in HR-002 §3.3 and §3.4. Relocation assistance, which is available only for company-initiated moves, is in HR-002 §8. Business travel is in FIN-001 and is a different thing entirely from working remotely.

## 2. Work Arrangements

### 2.1 The Three Arrangements

| Arrangement | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Office-based | Works from a Meridian office 4 or more days per week |
| Hybrid | Works from a Meridian office at least **2 days per week**, agreed with the manager |
| Fully remote | No regular office attendance requirement; may live anywhere on the approved list (§5.2) |

### 2.2 How It Is Set
The arrangement is stated in the offer letter and recorded in Workday. It is a property of the role as much as the person: some roles, such as those requiring physical access to lab hardware, are office-based and cannot be converted.

### 2.3 Changing Arrangement
A change requires manager approval and People Operations processing, effective the first day of a month. A change from Fully remote to Hybrid may require relocation, which the company will not fund for an employee-initiated change (HR-002 §8.3).

### 2.4 Hybrid Day Selection
Which two days are worked in the office is agreed within the team, not chosen unilaterally. Teams are encouraged to align days so that in-office time is actually collaborative. A manager may designate one fixed team day per week; beyond that, the second day is the employee's choice.

### 2.5 Eligibility for Allowances
Hybrid and Fully remote employees receive the home internet allowance (HR-003 §8). All three arrangements receive the home office stipend under §6, because office-based employees still work from home occasionally.

## 3. Working Hours

### 3.1 General Expectation
Meridian does not track hours for exempt employees. Non-exempt U.S. employees must record all hours worked (HR-001 §2.6).

### 3.2 Overlap Requirement
Every employee must have at least **4 hours of daily overlap** with the core hours of their team's designated anchor time zone. The anchor zone is set by the team's manager and recorded in the team charter. This is the only hard constraint on schedule; within it, employees arrange their day as they see fit.

### 3.3 Meeting Hours
Meetings should be scheduled within the overlap window. A meeting outside a participant's normal working day requires their agreement and should rotate if recurring, so the same people are not always inconvenienced.

### 3.4 Asynchronous Default
Decisions with cross-time-zone impact are documented in writing rather than settled verbally in a meeting that half the team cannot attend.

### 3.5 Right to Disconnect
Outside working hours, employees are not expected to monitor or respond to messages. The exception is an active on-call rotation (ENG-001), where response obligations are explicit and compensated. Employees of the German entity have additional statutory protections in this area; managers of German employees should familiarize themselves with the local addendum.

## 4. Working From Another Country Temporarily

### 4.1 What This Covers
Working in the ordinary course from a country other than the employee's country of employment, for a limited period — sometimes called "work from anywhere." It is not business travel (FIN-001) and it is not relocation (§5). No travel expenses are reimbursed for a work-from-anywhere arrangement; the employee bears all costs.

### 4.2 Annual Allowance
*(Amended effective 1 November 2025 — see POL-000 §3.3. The prior allowance was 20 days.)*

Up to **30 calendar days** per calendar year, in total across all countries.

### 4.3 Single-Country Limit
Within that 30-day allowance, no more than **20 calendar days** may be spent in any one country. This sub-limit exists to avoid creating a permanent establishment or a personal tax residency exposure, and it is not waivable by a manager.

### 4.4 Approval
Requires manager approval and a submission in Workday at least 21 days in advance, stating the country and exact dates. Requests involving sanctioned jurisdictions, or countries where Meridian has no entity and an existing tax exposure, are declined by Finance.

### 4.5 Conditions
The overlap requirement in §3.2 continues to apply and is not relaxed because of the time difference the employee has chosen. Equipment must remain in the employee's possession and comply with SEC-001; some countries are excluded on security grounds regardless of tax status. Business travel insurance (HR-003 §9.3) does **not** cover work-from-anywhere periods; employees should arrange their own cover.

### 4.6 Days That Do Not Count
Days on approved business travel (FIN-001) do not count against the 30-day allowance. Days of paid time off spent abroad do not count, provided the employee is not working. A day on which any work is performed counts in full.

## 5. Permanent Relocation

### 5.1 Approval Required Before Committing
An employee who wishes to move their home location permanently must obtain approval **before** signing a lease, giving notice on a property, or booking a move. Approval is not a formality: it depends on entity presence, tax and employment law, salary zone impact, and team overlap requirements.

### 5.2 Approved Locations
Employees may be employed only in a country where Meridian has a legal entity — the United States, Ireland, Germany, Canada, and Singapore — or through the employer-of-record arrangement in the small number of additional countries Finance has enabled. The current list is maintained by People Operations and changes as entity and EOR coverage changes; ask before assuming a country is available.

### 5.3 Consequences
A relocation may change the salary zone and therefore base salary (HR-002 §3.3, §3.4), the applicable benefits package (HR-003), the leave entitlement (HR-004 §3), and the notice period (HR-007 §2.1). All of these follow the new location, not the old one.

### 5.4 Financial Assistance
Employee-initiated relocation carries **no** financial assistance. Relocation assistance under HR-002 §8 is available only where Meridian initiates the move.

### 5.5 Within-Country Moves
A move within the same country still requires notification so that payroll, tax withholding, and the salary zone are correct, but does not require pre-approval unless it changes the work arrangement in §2.

## 6. Home Office Stipend

### 6.1 Initial Setup
A one-time stipend of **USD 750** for home office setup, available to all employees regardless of arrangement, claimable from the start date.

### 6.2 Refresh
A further **USD 300** every 36 months from the date of the initial claim, for replacement or upgrade of home office furniture.

### 6.3 What It Covers
Desk, chair, lighting, and ergonomic accessories. It does not cover computing hardware or peripherals, which come from IT-001 §3 and §4, and it does not cover internet connectivity, which is the allowance in HR-003 §8. These are three separate budgets with three separate owners, and they cannot be pooled.

### 6.4 Ownership
Items purchased with the home office stipend belong to the employee and are **not** returnable on termination. This is the opposite of the rule for IT equipment (HR-007 §7.1), and the distinction is deliberate: a chair is the employee's, a laptop is not.

### 6.5 Ergonomic Assessment
An employee experiencing discomfort may request a remote ergonomic assessment through People Operations. Equipment recommended by an assessment is funded separately and does not consume the §6.1 or §6.2 amounts.

## 7. Office Use

### 7.1 Desks
Meridian offices operate on a booked-desk basis. Book through the office system at least one day in advance. Office-based employees may request a permanently assigned desk.

### 7.2 Visitors
Visitors sign in and are escorted. Employees may not bring visitors into areas where Restricted data is displayed (SEC-001 §3.4).

### 7.3 Office Days for Remote Employees
Fully remote employees are welcome in any office and should book a desk. Travel to an office for a normal working day is not reimbursable; travel for an agreed team gathering is, under FIN-001.
