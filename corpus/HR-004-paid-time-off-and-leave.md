# HR-004 — Paid Time Off and Leave

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document covers every category of leave at Meridian: vacation and paid time off, company holidays, sick leave, parental leave, bereavement, jury duty, unpaid leave, and the sabbatical program. It applies to employees of all Meridian legal entities. Contractors and interns are addressed in §9.

### 1.2 A Note on Amendments
Section 4.2 of this document was amended effective 1 January 2026. The amended text appears below, and the change is recorded in POL-000 §3. If you have an older PDF copy of this handbook, the carryover figures in it are out of date.

## 2. Company Holidays

### 2.1 Entitlement
Meridian observes 11 paid company holidays in the United States, 10 in Ireland, 10 in Germany (varying by state of employment), 10 in Canada, and 11 in Singapore. The observed dates for each location are published each November for the following year.

### 2.2 Floating Days
In addition, every employee receives **2 floating holidays** per calendar year, to be used for observances not on the published list. Floating days do not carry over and are not paid out on termination.

### 2.3 Holiday Falling During Leave
A company holiday that falls on a day the employee would otherwise have taken as paid time off is charged as a holiday, not deducted from the PTO balance.

## 3. Paid Time Off — Accrual

### 3.1 United States, Canada, and Singapore
Employees accrue paid time off at a rate determined by **tenure tier**, as defined in HR-001 §4. The tier is not a function of job level (ORG-002 §4).

| Tenure tier | Annual PTO | Accrual per semi-monthly pay period (US/Canada) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier A | 15 days | 0.625 days |
| Tier B | 20 days | 0.833 days |
| Tier C | 25 days | 1.042 days |

Accrual begins on the start date. The accrual rate changes on the effective date of the tenure tier change (HR-001 §4.2), not retroactively to the anniversary.

### 3.2 Ireland
Employees of Meridian Systems Ireland Ltd. receive a flat **25 days** of annual leave: the statutory 20 days plus 5 company days. This entitlement does **not** vary by tenure tier. Annual leave is granted at the start of the leave year rather than accrued per pay period, subject to pro-ration in the year of hire or departure.

### 3.3 Germany
Employees of Meridian Systems GmbH receive a flat **28 days** of annual leave, which does not vary by tenure tier and is granted at the start of the leave year.

### 3.4 Part-Time Pro-Ration
Regular part-time employees (HR-001 §2.2) receive the entitlement for their tier or country, pro-rated by scheduled hours against a 40-hour week. An employee scheduled 24 hours per week in Tier B receives 12 days (20 × 24/40).

### 3.5 Requesting Time Off
Requests are submitted in Workday and require manager approval. Requests of 5 or more consecutive business days should be submitted at least 15 calendar days in advance. Approval may be declined for business reasons, but a manager who declines a request must offer an alternative window within 60 days.

### 3.6 Negative Balances
Employees may go up to 5 days negative against future accrual with manager approval. A negative balance outstanding at termination is recovered from final pay where local law permits.

## 4. Paid Time Off — Carryover and Payout

### 4.1 General Principle
Paid time off is intended to be taken in the year it is earned. Carryover is permitted but limited.

### 4.2 Carryover Limit
*(Amended effective 1 January 2026 — see POL-000 §3.1. The prior rule permitted 5 days carryover expiring 31 March.)*

An employee may carry over a maximum of **10 days** of unused paid time off into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **30 June** of the following year, after which they expire without payment. Balances above the 10-day cap on 31 December are forfeited and are not paid out.

For the avoidance of doubt: the 10-day cap and the 30 June expiry apply to the U.S., Canada, and Singapore entitlements in §3.1. Ireland and Germany carryover is governed by §4.3.

### 4.3 Ireland and Germany Carryover
Irish statutory leave must generally be taken within the leave year; Meridian permits carryover of up to 5 days of the company portion into the following year, to be used by 31 March. German statutory leave may be carried into the following year and must be taken by 31 March, per §7 Bundesurlaubsgesetz, and Meridian applies the same deadline to the full 28-day entitlement.

### 4.4 Payout on Termination
Accrued and unused paid time off is paid out on termination in all locations. Floating holidays (§2.2) and carried-over days that have already expired are not paid out. Sabbatical eligibility (§8) has no cash value.

## 5. Sick Leave

### 5.1 United States, Canada, and Singapore
10 paid sick days per calendar year, granted in full on 1 January rather than accrued. Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out on termination. Where a state or provincial law provides a more generous entitlement, that law applies.

### 5.2 Ireland and Germany
Statutory sick pay applies. Meridian tops up statutory payments to 100% of base salary for the first 8 weeks of a continuous absence, after which the disability coverage in HR-003 §9.2 applies.

### 5.3 Certification
A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days in all locations, and earlier where local law or the employment contract requires it.

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Entitlement
Meridian provides paid parental leave to all regular employees globally on the birth, adoption, or placement of a child, in addition to any statutory entitlement.

| Role | Paid leave |
| --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks |
| Secondary caregiver | 8 weeks |

### 6.2 Eligibility
The employee must have **12 completed months** of continuous service (HR-001 §4.1) at the expected date of birth or placement. This 12-month requirement is separate from and unrelated to the tenure tiers in §3.1; an employee at 13 months of service is eligible for full parental leave while still being in Tier A for PTO accrual purposes.

### 6.3 Interaction with Statutory Leave
Where statutory leave exists — Irish maternity and parent's leave, German Elternzeit and Mutterschutz, Canadian EI parental benefits — Meridian's paid leave runs concurrently with it and tops payment up to 100% of base salary. Meridian's entitlement is not additional to statutory leave except where local law requires.

### 6.4 Taking the Leave
Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within 12 months of the birth or placement. Each block must be at least two consecutive weeks. Notice of at least 30 days is required for each block where foreseeable.

### 6.5 Effect on Other Entitlements
Paid parental leave counts as continuous service for tenure tier purposes (HR-001 §4.3) and as employed time for bonus pro-ration (HR-002 §5.3). Paid time off continues to accrue during paid parental leave. It does not accrue during unpaid leave under §7.

## 7. Other Leave

### 7.1 Bereavement
Up to 5 paid days on the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or equivalent in-law relation), and up to 2 paid days for other close relationships. Additional unpaid time may be approved by the manager.

### 7.2 Jury Duty and Civic Obligations
Paid time off for the full duration of jury service. Employees should forward the summons to People Operations. Where the court pays a stipend, the employee keeps it.

### 7.3 Unpaid Personal Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 8 weeks may be approved by the manager and People Operations for personal circumstances not covered elsewhere in this document. Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave, with the employee responsible for their share of premiums. Paid time off does not accrue during unpaid leave, and continuous service is preserved (HR-001 §4.3).

### 7.4 Military Leave
Granted as required by applicable law in each jurisdiction.

## 8. Sabbatical

### 8.1 Entitlement
An employee who reaches **Tenure Tier C** (HR-001 §4.1 — 61 or more completed months of continuous service) is eligible for a one-time paid sabbatical of **4 consecutive weeks**, in addition to their annual PTO entitlement.

### 8.2 Conditions
The sabbatical must be taken as a single continuous block, requires 90 days' notice, and requires manager and skip-level approval to schedule. It cannot be split, deferred indefinitely, converted to pay, or carried into a payout at termination. An employee who does not take the sabbatical does not accrue a second one on reaching a later service milestone; the entitlement is one per employment.

### 8.3 Exclusions
Fixed-term employees (HR-001 §2.3) are not eligible. Rehired employees start a new service clock (HR-001 §4.3) and must reach 61 months of new continuous service to qualify, regardless of a sabbatical taken during prior employment.

## 9. Contractors and Interns

### 9.1 Contractors
Contractors do not accrue paid time off and are not covered by any section of this document. Time-off arrangements are between the contractor and their agency.

### 9.2 Interns
Interns receive paid company holidays (§2.1) and floating holidays pro-rated to the length of the internship, but do not accrue paid time off under §3 and are not eligible for parental leave or sabbatical.
