# HR-002 — Compensation and Payroll

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document covers base salary, pay frequency, geographic differentials, the annual compensation review, bonus, referral awards, equity, and relocation assistance. It applies to employees of all Meridian legal entities. It does not apply to contractors (HR-001 §2.5), whose rates are set in the applicable statement of work.

### 1.2 Related Documents
Job levels are defined in ORG-002. On-call compensation is **not** in this document — see §6 below and ENG-001 §5. Travel reimbursement is not compensation; see FIN-001.

## 2. Pay Frequency and Pay Dates

### 2.1 United States and Canada
Semi-monthly, paid on the 15th and the last calendar day of each month. Where a pay date falls on a weekend or a company holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

### 2.2 Ireland, Germany, and Singapore
Monthly, paid on the 25th of the month for that month. Where the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

### 2.3 Payslips
Payslips are available in Workday no later than the pay date. Retain them; Meridian is not able to reissue payslips for periods more than 24 months old for employees of the Irish and German entities, per the applicable retention schedule.

### 2.4 Payroll Corrections
Report a suspected payroll error to `payroll@meridiansystems.example` within 60 days of the affected pay date. Underpayments of more than one day's pay are corrected by off-cycle payment within five business days. Smaller corrections are made in the next scheduled run. Overpayments are recovered by agreed schedule; where local law requires written consent to deduct, that consent is obtained first.

## 3. Salary Bands

### 3.1 Structure
Each job level (ORG-002 §3) has a salary band with a minimum, midpoint, and maximum expressed in the local currency of the employing entity. Bands are reviewed annually against market survey data and republished each January. Bands themselves are not published to all employees; your own band is visible to you in Workday and your manager can discuss it with you.

### 3.2 Position in Band
A new hire is normally placed between the band minimum and the midpoint. Placement above midpoint at hire requires People Operations approval; placement above the band maximum is not permitted.

### 3.3 Geographic Differentials
Base salary is adjusted by a location factor applied to the reference band.

| Zone | Representative locations | Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | San Francisco Bay Area, New York City, London, Zurich | 1.15 |
| Zone 2 | Austin, Dublin, Toronto, Berlin, Singapore, Amsterdam, Chicago | 1.00 |
| Zone 3 | All other approved locations | 0.90 |

### 3.4 Relocation and Zone Changes
An employee who relocates to a different zone has their base salary adjusted to the new zone effective the first day of the month following the relocation date. A move from Zone 1 to Zone 3 therefore reduces base salary. Employees considering relocation should model this before committing, and must obtain approval under WRK-001 §5 before relocating; approval of the location and adjustment of the salary are separate steps.

### 3.5 Zone Is Not the Same as City Tier
The zones in §3.3 govern **salary**. A separate and differently-defined **city tier** system governs per diem and hotel caps for business travel (FIN-001 §5). The two systems use different city groupings and must not be used interchangeably. Singapore, for example, is Zone 2 for salary but Tier 1 for travel per diem.

## 4. Annual Compensation Review

### 4.1 Cycle
The annual compensation review runs in February and March. Changes take effect on **1 April** and appear in the first pay run on or after that date.

### 4.2 Inputs
Merit increases are a function of the performance rating from the most recent completed review cycle (HR-005 §4), current position in band (§3.2), and the merit budget allocated to the function.

### 4.3 Off-Cycle Increases
Off-cycle base salary increases are made only for promotions (HR-005 §6), for corrections of demonstrable band or equity errors, and for statutory minimum adjustments. Retention offers require executive approval.

## 5. Bonus

### 5.1 Target Percentages
Employees in eligible roles have an annual bonus target expressed as a percentage of base salary earned during the plan year.

| Level | Target |
| --- | --- |
| IC1 – IC4 / M1 | 10% |
| IC5 – IC7 / M2 – M3 | 15% |
| M4 – M5 | 20% |

Sales roles are not covered by this plan; they participate in a separate variable compensation plan documented in the individual commission agreement.

### 5.2 Payout Conditions
Bonus is paid annually in March for the preceding plan year. Payout is a function of company attainment and individual performance rating. An employee must be employed and not under notice on the payout date to receive a bonus, except where local law requires otherwise, which is the case in Germany for pro-rata entitlement in certain circumstances.

### 5.3 Pro-Ration
Employees hired during a plan year receive a pro-rated target based on days employed. Time spent on paid parental leave (HR-004 §6) counts as employed time for bonus pro-ration purposes.

## 6. On-Call Compensation

### 6.1 Where It Is Defined
On-call compensation is **not** set by this document. Rates, eligibility, and the treatment of incident hours are defined in ENG-001 §5, and differ materially between the U.S. entity and the EU entities. Payroll processes on-call payments based on the rotation records submitted by Engineering Operations.

### 6.2 Payment Timing
Where on-call compensation is payable under ENG-001 §5, it is paid in the second pay run of the month following the month in which the rotation occurred.

## 7. Referral Awards

### 7.1 Amount
A referral award of USD 3,000 (or local currency equivalent at the rate published each January) is payable for a referral that results in a regular full-time hire.

### 7.2 Conditions
The referrer must be a Meridian employee at the time of referral and at the time of payment, must have submitted the referral through the applicant tracking system before the candidate applied independently, and must not be the hiring manager, the recruiter, or in the reporting chain above the hiring manager. Employees still in the probationary period (HR-001 §3) may submit referrals but the award is paid only after the referrer's own probation completes.

### 7.3 Timing
The award is paid after the referred employee completes 90 days of employment.

### 7.4 Exclusions
No award is payable for intern, contractor, or fixed-term hires, or for rehires of former Meridian employees.

## 8. Relocation Assistance

### 8.1 What This Section Covers
This section covers assistance with a **permanent change of the employee's home location**, whether at hire or during employment. It is distinct from business travel, which is reimbursed under FIN-001. Do not submit relocation costs as travel expenses; they are processed differently for tax purposes and a misfiled claim will be rejected.

### 8.2 Amounts
Relocation assistance is paid as a lump sum, grossed up for tax where required by local law.

| Move type | Lump sum |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic (within the same country) | USD 12,000 |
| International (across countries) | USD 20,000 |

### 8.3 Eligibility and Approval
Relocation assistance is offered at the company's initiative — for example, where Meridian asks an employee to move, or where it forms part of a hire package. It is **not** available for employee-initiated relocations made for personal reasons; those are governed by WRK-001 §5 and carry no financial assistance, though they may carry a salary adjustment under §3.4 of this document.

### 8.4 Repayment
Relocation assistance is repayable on a sliding scale if the employee resigns within 24 months: 100% within 12 months, 50% between 13 and 24 months, nil thereafter.

## 9. Equity

### 9.1 Grants
Regular employees at IC2 and above are eligible for restricted stock unit grants. New-hire grants are approved by the board at the next scheduled meeting following the start date.

### 9.2 Vesting
Standard vesting is four years with a one-year cliff, vesting quarterly thereafter. Vesting is contingent on continued service; see HR-007 §5 for treatment at termination.

### 9.3 Refresh Grants
Refresh grants are considered annually in the compensation review cycle (§4) for employees who have completed at least one year of service. Refresh grants are discretionary and are not guaranteed by prior grants.
