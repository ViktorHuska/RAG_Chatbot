# POL-000 — Handbook Governance, Amendments, and Changelog

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document governs the handbook itself: how documents are versioned, how amendments are made, and — most importantly — it is the authoritative record of every amendment made to any handbook document.

### 1.2 Precedence
**Where an amendment recorded in §3 of this document conflicts with the text of the amended document, this document prevails.** Individual documents are re-issued periodically to absorb their amendments, but between re-issues the changelog here is authoritative. Always check this document before relying on a specific figure, threshold, rate, or date found elsewhere in the handbook.

### 1.3 Precedence Generally
Where any two handbook documents conflict and no amendment is involved, the following order applies, highest first: (1) applicable law and the individual employment contract; (2) SEC-001, on any question of security or data handling; (3) the more specific document over the more general; (4) the more recently effective document. ORG-001 is descriptive and never overrides a policy document.

## 2. Amendment Process

### 2.1 Who May Amend
Only the document owner named in the document's front matter may propose an amendment. People Operations records it here. An amendment affecting more than one document requires the agreement of every affected owner.

### 2.2 Notice
Amendments that reduce an entitlement take effect no sooner than 30 days after announcement, and longer where local law requires consultation. Amendments that increase an entitlement may take effect immediately.

### 2.3 Announcement
All amendments are announced in the `#handbook` channel and in the monthly People Operations digest. Employees are responsible for reading the digest; a change is effective on its stated date whether or not any individual has read it.

### 2.4 Version Numbering
A document's major version increments on a substantive rewrite; the minor version increments on an amendment. The `effective` date in the front matter is the date of the most recent change to that document.

## 3. Amendment Log

Amendments are listed most recent first. Each entry states the affected document and section, the change, and the effective date.

### 3.1 HR-004 §4.2 — Paid time off carryover increased
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The maximum paid time off carried from one calendar year into the next increased from **5 days to 10 days**, and the deadline for using carried-over days moved from **31 March to 30 June** of the following year.

This applies to the entitlements in HR-004 §3.1 — the United States, Canada, and Singapore. It does **not** change Irish or German carryover, which remain governed by HR-004 §4.3 and by statute.

Any handbook copy, onboarding deck, or manager guidance stating "5 days, expiring 31 March" is out of date.

### 3.2 FIN-001 §5.1 — Tier 1 meal per diem increased
**Effective 1 April 2026.** The Tier 1 city meal per diem increased from **USD 95 to USD 110** per day. Tier 2 (USD 75) and Tier 3 (USD 60) rates are unchanged, as are all hotel caps in FIN-001 §5.2.

The rate applying to a claim is the rate in force on the **date of travel**, not the date the claim is submitted or approved. A trip taken in March 2026 and claimed in April 2026 is reimbursed at USD 95 per day.

### 3.3 WRK-001 §4.2 — Work-from-anywhere allowance increased
**Effective 1 November 2025.** The annual allowance for working from another country increased from **20 to 30 calendar days** per calendar year.

The single-country sub-limit in WRK-001 §4.3 was **not** changed and remains 20 calendar days in any one country. An employee may therefore now split 30 days across at least two countries, but still may not spend more than 20 days in one.

### 3.4 IT-001 §3.1 — Linux laptop option added
**Effective 15 February 2026.** A supported Ubuntu LTS option on Dell Latitude hardware was added for the Platform and Infrastructure Engineering job family, on request. The refresh interval for this machine is 36 months, matching the other engineering families in IT-001 §3.2. Windows remains unsupported as an employee platform.

### 3.5 HR-006 §3.1 — Learning budget for senior levels increased
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The annual learning and development budget for IC6–IC7 and M3–M5 increased from USD 4,000 to **USD 5,000**. Budgets for IC1–IC3 (USD 2,000) and IC4–IC5/M1–M2 (USD 3,000) are unchanged. The carryover rule in HR-006 §3.3 is unchanged: 50% of unused budget to a cap of USD 1,000, expiring 31 December of the following year.

### 3.6 ENG-001 §3.3 — On-call frequency cap tightened
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The maximum primary on-call frequency changed from one week in three to **one week in four**. Teams unable to meet the cap must escalate to Engineering Operations rather than operate outside it.

### 3.7 HR-003 §5.2 — Ireland pension contribution restructured
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The employer PRSA contribution for Irish employees changed from a flat 6% of base salary to a **tenure-tiered schedule**: 5% for Tenure Tier A, 7% for Tier B, and 9% for Tier C, as defined in HR-001 §4. Employees at Tier A saw a reduction; the change was announced in September 2025 to satisfy the 30-day notice requirement in §2.2 with margin.

### 3.8 SEC-001 §5.3 — AI tool rules clarified
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The prior text prohibited "sensitive data" in AI tools without defining the term. The amended text distinguishes Confidential data, permitted only in tools on the approved list, from Restricted data including all customer data, which is prohibited in any AI tool without exception.

### 3.9 FIN-001 §7.2 — Expense submission deadline shortened
**Effective 1 January 2026.** The deadline for submitting travel expense claims shortened from **60 days to 30 days** from the expense date. The separate 60-day deadline for learning budget claims in HR-006 §8.2 was not changed, which is why the two now differ.

## 5. Questions and Corrections

### 5.1 Reporting an Error
If you find a contradiction between two handbook documents, or a figure that does not match what you have been told, report it in the `#handbook` channel or to People Operations. Do not resolve it by guessing which document is right; §1.3 gives the precedence order, but a genuine contradiction is a defect to be fixed rather than navigated.

### 5.2 Interpretation
Where a policy is ambiguous, the document owner's interpretation governs, and the resulting clarification is recorded as an amendment here so the next person does not have to ask.
