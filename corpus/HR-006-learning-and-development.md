# HR-006 — Learning and Development Budget

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document describes the annual per-employee learning and development budget, what it may be spent on, how it is approved and claimed, and how it interacts with travel policy when the spend involves attending an event in person.

### 1.2 Related Documents
Travel booked in connection with a learning activity is governed by FIN-001 in full. Job levels referenced below are defined in ORG-002 §3.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who Has a Budget
All regular full-time and regular part-time employees who have completed their probationary period (HR-001 §3) have an annual learning budget. Part-time employees receive the full amount, not pro-rated.

### 2.2 Who Does Not
Interns, contractors, and fixed-term employees engaged for less than 12 months do not have a learning budget. Fixed-term employees engaged for 12 months or more receive the budget pro-rated to the contract term.

## 3. Budget Amounts

### 3.1 By Level
The annual budget is keyed to **job level** (ORG-002 §3), not to tenure tier.

| Level | Annual budget |
| --- | --- |
| IC1 – IC3 | USD 2,000 |
| IC4 – IC5 / M1 – M2 | USD 3,000 |
| IC6 – IC7 / M3 – M5 | USD 5,000 |

Local currency equivalents are set each January using the same reference rate as HR-002 §7.1.

### 3.2 Budget Year
The budget year is the calendar year. An employee who is promoted mid-year moves to the higher amount immediately, with any spend already made in the year counting against the new, higher total. An employee hired mid-year receives the full annual amount, not a pro-rated portion, provided they complete probation before 1 October; those completing probation on or after 1 October receive 50%.

### 3.3 Carryover
Up to **50% of the unused budget, capped at USD 1,000**, carries into the following calendar year. Carried amounts must be spent by **31 December** of that following year and cannot be carried a second time.

Note that this is a different rule from the paid time off carryover in HR-004 §4.2, which uses a 10-day cap and a 30 June expiry. The two are frequently confused. Learning budget carryover expires at the end of the following year; PTO carryover expires halfway through it.

## 4. Eligible Spend

### 4.1 Generally Approved
The following require manager approval only:

- Conference and workshop registration fees
- Online course subscriptions and individual course purchases
- Professional certification fees, including one retake per certification
- Technical and business books, in any format
- Professional association or society membership dues
- Language tuition where the language is used in the employee's role
- Coaching from a credentialed coach, up to USD 1,500 per year

### 4.2 Requires Additional Approval
The following require manager approval **and** People Operations approval:

- Degree programs and multi-year academic courses, which additionally require a study agreement (§6)
- Any single item above USD 2,500
- Coaching above the USD 1,500 annual limit in §4.1
- Equipment where the learning case is the primary justification (see §5.2)

### 4.3 Not Eligible
- Hardware and peripherals for general work use — these come from IT-001 §3 and §4, or from the home office stipend in WRK-001 §6
- Team offsites, team lunches, and social events, which come from the manager's team budget
- Travel, accommodation, and meals — see §5.1
- Products or subscriptions Meridian already licenses company-wide; check with IT before purchasing

## 5. Learning Spend That Involves Travel

### 5.1 What Comes From Where
Where an employee attends a conference or course in person, the split is:

| Cost | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Registration or tuition fee | Learning budget (this document) |
| Flights, rail, and ground transport | Travel budget, per FIN-001 |
| Accommodation | Travel budget, per FIN-001 §5.2 hotel caps |
| Meals | Travel budget, per FIN-001 §5.1 per diem |

The learning budget is therefore not consumed by travel costs. However, the **travel itself** requires separate approval under FIN-001 §3 — approval of the learning spend does not approve the trip. Employees regularly make the mistake of getting a conference ticket approved and booking flights on the assumption the trip is authorized; it is not.

### 5.2 Equipment Purchased for Learning
Where a course requires specific hardware — a development board, a lab kit — it may be charged to the learning budget under §4.2 with People Operations approval. The item remains Meridian property and is subject to the return obligations in IT-001 §6 if it exceeds USD 500 in value.

## 6. Study Agreements

### 6.1 When Required
Any learning spend exceeding **USD 5,000 in a single commitment** — typically a degree program or an extended certification track — requires a written study agreement signed before the spend is committed.

### 6.2 Repayment Terms
A study agreement includes a repayment obligation if the employee resigns within 24 months of the course completing: 100% within 12 months, 50% between 13 and 24 months, nil thereafter. This mirrors the relocation repayment schedule in HR-002 §8.4 but is a separate obligation; an employee may be subject to both.

### 6.3 Approval
Study agreements are approved by the functional executive and People Operations.

## 7. Learning Time

### 7.1 Allowance
Employees may use up to **4 hours per month** of working time for self-directed learning, with manager awareness. This is not tracked formally and does not accumulate month to month.

### 7.2 Conference Attendance
Days spent attending an approved conference are working days, not paid time off. Travel days to and from an event are likewise working days.

### 7.3 Study Leave
Time off for examinations associated with an approved certification or degree is granted as paid time, up to 3 days per calendar year, and is not deducted from the PTO balance in HR-004 §3.

## 8. Claiming

### 8.1 Process
Obtain approval first, then purchase, then claim through the expense system with a receipt and the approval reference. Pre-approval is mandatory: the learning budget does not reimburse retroactively approved spend.

### 8.2 Deadline
Claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense date and in all cases before 15 January following the budget year. Note that this differs from the 30-day submission deadline for travel expenses in FIN-001 §7.2.

### 8.3 Direct Billing
For registration fees above USD 1,000, request direct company billing through Finance rather than paying personally and claiming back.
