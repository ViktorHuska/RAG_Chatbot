# HR-008 — Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This code applies to every person doing work for Meridian Systems — employees, contractors, interns, and board members — in every location, and to conduct at work, at work-adjacent events, in company systems, and in public channels where the person is identifiable as associated with Meridian.

### 1.2 Enforcement
Breach of this code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or engagement, and in some cases referral to law enforcement or a regulator.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

### 2.1 The Standard
Treat colleagues, customers, partners, and candidates with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume competence and good faith until you have specific evidence otherwise, and when you do have that evidence, raise it directly rather than through implication.

### 2.2 Inclusion
Meridian does not tolerate discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, colour, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, pregnancy, marital or family status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

### 2.3 Harassment
Harassment is unwelcome conduct that a reasonable person would find intimidating, hostile, or degrading. It does not require intent to cause harm, and it does not require the target to have objected at the time. Sexual harassment includes unwelcome advances, requests for sexual favours, and conduct of a sexual nature that affects the work environment.

### 2.4 Retaliation
Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is itself a serious breach of this code and is treated as such regardless of whether the underlying concern is substantiated.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

### 3.1 Disclosure Obligation
Disclose any circumstance where personal interest could reasonably appear to influence a Meridian decision. Disclosure is to the manager and to Legal (`legal@meridiansystems.example`). Disclosing early is never penalized; failing to disclose is.

### 3.2 Common Situations Requiring Disclosure
- A close personal relationship with someone in your reporting chain, or with a person you are involved in hiring, evaluating, or setting compensation for
- Financial interest in a customer, supplier, partner, or competitor beyond holdings in a diversified fund
- Outside employment, advisory roles, or board seats, including unpaid ones
- A family member or partner employed by a customer, supplier, or competitor
- Personal benefit from a supplier selection decision

### 3.3 Relationships at Work
Consensual relationships between colleagues are not prohibited, but a relationship within a reporting line — direct or indirect — must be disclosed, and the reporting line will be changed. Neither party is presumed to be at fault; failing to disclose is the breach.

### 3.4 Outside Work
Outside paid work is permitted if it does not compete with Meridian, does not use Meridian time, systems, or confidential information, and does not create an availability conflict including on-call obligations (ENG-001 §4). It must be disclosed in advance and approved in writing.

## 4. Gifts, Hospitality, and Anti-Corruption

### 4.1 Threshold
Gifts and hospitality with a value under **USD 100** may be accepted or given without approval, provided they are infrequent, unsolicited, and would not embarrass Meridian if made public. Anything above that threshold requires prior approval from Legal.

### 4.2 Never Acceptable
Cash or cash equivalents in any amount. Anything offered in connection with a pending decision — a contract award, a renewal, a hiring decision. Anything offered to a government official, at any value, without prior Legal approval.

### 4.3 Anti-Bribery
Meridian is subject to the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, the U.K. Bribery Act, and equivalent laws in the jurisdictions where it operates. Facilitation payments are prohibited without exception, including where they are customary in a local market.

### 4.4 Reporting Gifts
Gifts received above the §4.1 threshold that could not be declined without causing offence must be reported to Legal within 5 business days and are normally donated or raffled internally.

## 5. Use of Company Systems

### 5.1 Reasonable Personal Use
Incidental personal use of company systems is permitted. Systems remain Meridian property and use may be monitored to the extent permitted by local law. In Germany, monitoring is subject to works council agreement and employees are notified of the scope in the local addendum.

### 5.2 Prohibited Use
Do not use company systems to store or transmit unlawful material, to run a personal business, to access customer data without a business need (SEC-001 §4), or to install software from unverified sources (IT-001 §7).

### 5.3 Artificial Intelligence Tools
Use of AI assistants is permitted and encouraged for tasks where it is appropriate, subject to SEC-001 §5.3: never paste Confidential or Restricted data into a tool that is not on the approved list, and review generated output before relying on it. The employee remains accountable for anything they ship.

## 6. Public Communication

### 6.1 Speaking About Meridian
Only designated spokespeople speak on behalf of Meridian to press or analysts. Route enquiries to `press@meridiansystems.example`.

### 6.2 Personal Channels
Employees are free to write and speak publicly, including about the industry Meridian operates in. Make clear you speak for yourself. Do not disclose non-public information about Meridian, its customers, its financials, or its roadmap. Do not name a customer without written permission from that customer.

### 6.3 Customer References
Any public reference to a customer relationship — a logo, a quote, a case study, a conference talk — requires written customer approval routed through Marketing.

## 7. Substances

### 7.1 Alcohol
Alcohol at company events is permitted where legal and where non-alcoholic options are equally available. Being impaired at work is a conduct issue. Managers must not create pressure to drink.

### 7.2 Drugs
Being under the influence of illegal drugs while working is prohibited. Employees with a substance dependency are encouraged to use the Employee Assistance Program (HR-003 §10), and doing so proactively is not a disciplinary matter.

## 8. Raising Concerns

### 8.1 Routes
Concerns may be raised with any of: your manager, your skip-level manager, People Operations, Legal, or the confidential ethics line operated by an independent third party, available in every location and in local languages. The ethics line accepts anonymous reports where local law permits.

### 8.2 Handling
Concerns are investigated proportionately and promptly. The reporter is told the outcome in general terms; detail about action taken in respect of another individual is not shared.

### 8.3 Good Faith
This code protects reports made in good faith, including reports that turn out to be mistaken. It does not protect knowingly false reports, which are themselves a breach.

### 8.4 Security Incidents Are Different
A suspected security incident — lost device, phishing, exposed credential, suspected breach — is **not** reported through this route. It goes to the security channel described in SEC-001 §7.1, within one hour, and reporting is mandatory rather than discretionary.
