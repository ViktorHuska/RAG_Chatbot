# HR-003 — Employee Benefits

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document describes health, retirement, insurance, and stipend benefits by legal entity. Leave of any kind — vacation, sick, parental, bereavement, sabbatical — is **not** covered here; all leave is in HR-004.

### 1.2 Plan Year
The benefits plan year matches the fiscal year (ORG-001 §2.4): 1 January to 31 December. Open enrolment runs for two weeks each November.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Regular Full-Time Employees
Eligible for all benefits described in this document, subject to the waiting periods stated in each section.

### 2.2 Regular Part-Time Employees
Eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage on the same employer contribution terms as full-time employees. Eligible for retirement plan participation. **Not** eligible for the wellness stipend (§7) or the home internet allowance (§8).

### 2.3 Fixed-Term Employees
Eligible on the same terms as regular employees of equivalent scheduled hours.

### 2.4 Interns
Eligible only for statutory coverage and the wellness stipend (§7).

### 2.5 Contractors
Not eligible for any benefit in this document.

## 3. Health Coverage — United States

### 3.1 Effective Date
Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following the start date. An employee starting on 3 March is covered from 1 April. An employee starting on the first of a month is covered from that same day.

### 3.2 Medical Plans
Three plans are offered through the carrier network.

| Plan | Deductible (individual) | Out-of-pocket max | HSA eligible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core PPO | USD 1,500 | USD 5,000 | No |
| Plus PPO | USD 500 | USD 3,000 | No |
| Saver HDHP | USD 3,300 | USD 6,600 | Yes |

### 3.3 Employer Contribution
Meridian pays 90% of the employee-only premium and 70% of the premium for dependents, for any plan selected.

### 3.4 Health Savings Account
Employees electing the Saver HDHP receive an employer HSA contribution of USD 1,200 per year for individual coverage or USD 2,400 for family coverage, funded in equal amounts each pay period.

### 3.5 Dental and Vision
A single dental plan and a single vision plan are offered. Meridian pays 100% of the employee-only premium and 60% for dependents.

## 4. Retirement — United States

### 4.1 401(k) Plan
All U.S. employees are eligible to contribute from their first pay period. There is no waiting period for employee contributions.

### 4.2 Employer Match
Meridian matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation, per pay period. There is no true-up at year end, so an employee who front-loads contributions early in the year and stops will receive less match than one who contributes evenly. Employees planning to max out contributions should spread them across the full year.

### 4.3 Vesting
Employer match is immediately 100% vested.

## 5. Retirement — Ireland

### 5.1 Plan Type
Employees of Meridian Systems Ireland Ltd. participate in a PRSA arrangement with the company's nominated provider.

### 5.2 Employer Contribution
The employer contribution is a percentage of base salary and is **keyed to tenure tier** as defined in HR-001 §4:

| Tenure tier | Employer contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Tier A | 5% of base salary |
| Tier B | 7% of base salary |
| Tier C | 9% of base salary |

Contributions begin after six completed months of service. An employee's contribution rate changes on the same date their tenure tier changes (HR-001 §4.2). Employee contributions are voluntary and are not required to receive the employer contribution.

## 6. Retirement and Statutory Benefits — Germany and Singapore

### 6.1 Germany
Statutory social insurance contributions are made as required by law. In addition, Meridian contributes 5% of base salary to a betriebliche Altersvorsorge arrangement for all employees of Meridian Systems GmbH, from the start date, with no tenure-based variation and no waiting period.

### 6.2 Singapore
Central Provident Fund contributions are made at statutory rates for eligible employees. No supplementary employer pension arrangement is offered.

### 6.3 Canada
Meridian matches employee RRSP contributions up to 4% of base salary, on the same terms as the U.S. 401(k) match described in §4.2, including the absence of a year-end true-up.

## 7. Wellness Stipend

### 7.1 Amount
USD 75 per month, or local currency equivalent, available to all employees including interns and part-time employees, from the start date.

### 7.2 Eligible Uses
Gym and studio memberships, fitness equipment, mental health applications and therapy not covered by insurance, nutrition services, and sleep or meditation subscriptions.

### 7.3 Claiming
Submit through the expense system with a receipt. The stipend does not accumulate: an unclaimed month is forfeited at the end of the following month. There is no annual carryover.

## 8. Home Internet Allowance

### 8.1 Amount
USD 50 per month for employees whose work arrangement under WRK-001 §2 is Hybrid or Fully Remote. Office-based employees are not eligible.

### 8.2 Interaction with Home Office Equipment
This allowance covers connectivity only. Desk, chair, and peripherals are covered by the home office setup stipend in WRK-001 §6, and computing hardware is provided under IT-001 §3. These three budgets are separate and cannot be combined or transferred.

## 9. Insurance

### 9.1 Life and Accidental Death
Company-paid coverage of 2× base salary, to a maximum of USD 500,000, for all regular employees. Supplemental coverage may be purchased at open enrolment.

### 9.2 Disability
Short-term disability at 70% of base salary for up to 12 weeks, and long-term disability at 60% of base salary thereafter, subject to plan terms. In Ireland and Germany, statutory sick pay applies first and company coverage is coordinated with it.

### 9.3 Travel Insurance
Business travel accident and medical coverage applies automatically to all employees on approved company travel (FIN-001). No enrolment is required. This coverage does not extend to personal travel appended to a business trip beyond 48 hours either side, nor to work-from-anywhere arrangements under WRK-001 §4.

## 10. Employee Assistance Program

### 10.1 Availability
Confidential counselling, legal, and financial consultation services are available to all employees and their household members in all locations, at no cost, from the start date. Usage is not reported to Meridian in any identifiable form.
