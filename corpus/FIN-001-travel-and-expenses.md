# FIN-001 — Business Travel and Expenses

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This document governs **business travel** — travel undertaken at Meridian's request for a business purpose — and the reimbursement of expenses incurred on it. It applies to employees of all entities and, where the statement of work says so, to contractors.

### 1.2 What This Document Is Not
This document does **not** cover:

- **Relocation.** A permanent change of home location is HR-002 §8 (assistance, where the company initiates the move) and WRK-001 §5 (approval). Relocation costs submitted as travel expenses are rejected, because the two are treated differently for tax.
- **Work from anywhere.** Working from another country for personal reasons is WRK-001 §4 and is entirely at the employee's own cost.
- **Commuting.** Ordinary travel between home and the employee's normal office is never reimbursable, including for fully remote employees travelling to an office for a normal working day (WRK-001 §7.3).

### 1.3 Amendment Notice
The Tier 1 per diem rate in §5.1 changes on 1 April 2026. See POL-000 §3.2 and the note in §5.1 below.

## 2. Principles

### 2.1 Spend It Like It's Yours
Book the reasonable option, not the cheapest miserable one and not the most comfortable available one. Where a rule in this document does not cover a situation, apply that principle and document your reasoning in the claim.

### 2.2 Travel Is Optional More Often Than It Looks
Before booking, ask whether the outcome needs a person in a room. Meridian is a distributed company and treats travel as a deliberate expense rather than a default.

## 3. Approval

### 3.1 Pre-Approval Required
All business travel requires manager pre-approval in the travel system before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from a director-level (M3/IC6) or above approver.

### 3.2 Approval of an Event Is Not Approval of the Trip
Approval of a conference registration under HR-006 does **not** authorize the associated travel. The trip requires its own approval under §3.1. This is the single most common process error at Meridian.

### 3.3 Advance Booking
Book at least **14 days** in advance for domestic travel and **21 days** for international travel. Bookings inside those windows require an explanation in the approval request; they are not refused, but the pattern is reviewed.

### 3.4 Booking Channel
Book through the TravelHub portal. Bookings made outside TravelHub are reimbursed only where TravelHub was unavailable or could not offer a comparable itinerary, and the reason is documented in the claim.

## 4. Air and Rail

### 4.1 Rail Preference
Where a rail journey of under 5 hours substitutes for a flight, take the rail option. Standard class for journeys under 3 hours; first class permitted above 3 hours where the fare difference is under 25%.

### 4.2 Cabin Class
Cabin class is keyed to **job level** (ORG-002 §3) and to flight duration.

| Scheduled flight duration | Entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| Under 6 hours | Economy, all levels |
| 6 to 10 hours | Premium economy, all levels |
| Over 10 hours | Premium economy; **business class for M4/IC7 and above**, or for any employee where the flight arrives less than 12 hours before a customer-facing commitment |

Note that the M4/IC7 threshold includes IC7 but not IC6, per the track equivalence in ORG-002 §2.3. Business class is never reimbursable on flights under 10 hours regardless of level.

### 4.3 Fare Selection
Book the lowest logical fare within the entitlement, allowing a variance of up to USD 150 for an itinerary that avoids an overnight layover or a connection under 60 minutes.

### 4.4 Personal Travel Attached to a Business Trip
Permitted. Meridian reimburses the cost the trip would have had without the personal extension; the employee pays the difference and must provide a comparison quote taken on the day of booking. Note that business travel insurance (HR-003 §9.3) extends only 48 hours either side of the business portion.

### 4.5 Loyalty Programs
Employees keep airline and hotel loyalty benefits earned on business travel. Loyalty status must not influence the choice of carrier where it costs Meridian more than USD 100 on the itinerary.

## 5. Daily Costs

### 5.1 Meal Per Diem
Meals are reimbursed on a per diem basis, keyed to the **destination city tier** below. This tier system is specific to travel and is **not** the same as the salary zone system in HR-002 §3.3 — Singapore, for example, is salary Zone 2 but travel Tier 1.

| Tier | Cities | Per diem (to 31 Mar 2026) | Per diem (from 1 Apr 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | San Francisco, New York, London, Zurich, Singapore, Tokyo, Geneva | USD 95 | **USD 110** |
| Tier 2 | Austin, Dublin, Berlin, Toronto, Chicago, Amsterdam, Sydney | USD 75 | USD 75 |
| Tier 3 | All other destinations | USD 60 | USD 60 |

*(The Tier 1 increase is an amendment recorded in POL-000 §3.2. Tier 2 and Tier 3 rates are unchanged.)*

The per diem is claimed at the applicable rate without itemized receipts. Where a meal is provided — by the hotel, by a conference, or by a host — reduce the claim by 25% for breakfast, 35% for lunch, and 40% for dinner.

### 5.2 Accommodation
Hotels are booked through TravelHub within the nightly cap for the destination tier, excluding tax.

| Tier | Nightly cap |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | USD 325 |
| Tier 2 | USD 240 |
| Tier 3 | USD 180 |

Where no compliant room is available within the cap — a common situation during large conferences — book the closest compliant option and note the reason. Exceeding the cap by more than 30% requires director-level approval.

### 5.3 Ground Transport
Public transport, airport transfers, and ride-hailing are reimbursable. Rental cars require pre-approval and are reimbursed with the collision damage waiver, which should always be taken. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the applicable statutory or published rate for the country of travel.

### 5.4 Incidentals
Baggage fees for one checked bag, in-flight connectivity, visa and entry fees, vaccinations required for the destination, laundry on trips of 5 nights or more, and reasonable tips.

### 5.5 Not Reimbursable
Airline lounge access and memberships (except where included in an entitled fare), seat upgrades beyond the entitlement in §4.2, in-room minibar, hotel gym or spa charges, fines and penalties including parking and traffic, travel for a companion, and alcohol beyond a reasonable amount with a meal.

## 6. Customer Entertainment

### 6.1 Approval
Entertainment of customers or prospects requires pre-approval from the budget owner. The claim must list every attendee by name and company.

### 6.2 Limits
USD 150 per person including tax and service. Above that requires director-level approval before the event, not after.

### 6.3 Public Officials
Any hospitality involving a government official requires prior Legal approval regardless of value (HR-008 §4.2).

## 7. Claiming

### 7.1 Receipts
An itemized receipt is required for every expense over **USD 25**, except per diem meal claims under §5.1, which require no receipt. A card statement is not an itemized receipt.

### 7.2 Deadline
Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims submitted after 60 days are paid only with director approval and an explanation. Note this is a shorter deadline than the 60-day window for learning budget claims in HR-006 §8.2.

### 7.3 Currency
Claim in the currency paid. The expense system applies the card rate where a corporate card was used, or the published month-average rate for out-of-pocket spend.

### 7.4 Reimbursement Timing
Approved out-of-pocket claims are paid in the next payroll run for the employee's entity (HR-002 §2), separately identified from salary.

### 7.5 Corporate Cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year, and to all M2 and above. Card transactions must be reconciled with receipts within the same 30-day window in §7.2. Persistent non-reconciliation results in card suspension.

## 8. Travel Safety

### 8.1 Registration
International trips are automatically registered with the travel assistance provider through TravelHub. Do not disable this.

### 8.2 High-Risk Destinations
Travel to a destination rated high risk by the provider requires executive approval and a security briefing.

### 8.3 In an Emergency
The 24-hour assistance number is on the back of the travel card and in the TravelHub app. For a security or data incident while travelling, follow SEC-001 §7 in parallel.
